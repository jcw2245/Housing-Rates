The Demand For Housing is Currently Insolvable By Growing Prices and Lower Rates of Construction Workers

The demand for housing is on the rise, but so is pricing. This is to keep up with demand with the lack of construction workers to produce more houses.

Photo By Pixabay

By Jaylynn Ward

Housing is a problem that a lot of Americans are facing. This is because of the small number of houses that are built compared to the demand and the pricing that continues to grow. This makes it hard for individuals and families to be able to afford a home to live in.

The median household costs over $405,000 in 2024 for the whole market. This is about a $18,000 dollar increase from 2023. The largest real estate site, Zillow, tracks their pricing and the difference over time.

This also creates stress for realtors who are helping clients find homes as they have to deal with an unstable market filled with high prices, low inventory and declining sales.

The housing market creates uncertainty for multiple parties involved. The numbers of uncertainty seem to grow.

The average age to buy a home is 38 years old for first-time buyers, and 56 years old overall, which is a record high.

First-time buyers hit an all-time low with only 24% of the market.

38% of a family’s income is needed to make a mortgage payment on a single-family home in the U.S.

Along with the prices increasing for housing, houses are not being built for the demand that they are needed. This is due to several factors like the cost of housing supplies increasing, housing supplies wait times, zoning restrictions and labor shortages.

An influx in requests for housing increased after the Covid-19 pandemic, and the labor industry has not been able to keep up. The construction industry has seen an increase in unemployment rates, including the most recent months.

Construction workers for housing make up 3.4 million of the over 8 million workers within this industry, according to the most recent Construction Labor Market Report. This is not nearly enough for the number of housing that needs to be built for individuals in the U.S.
